# The 5-Signal ERP Readability Checklist

A practical checklist for founders, CFOs, COOs, finance leaders, and project directors who use legacy ERP systems but still depend on spreadsheets for everyday answers.

## The problem

Most ERPs are not missing data.

They are missing an accessible decision layer.

When finance and operations teams spend hours exporting, cleaning, joining, and interpreting ERP data, leadership loses time and visibility. This checklist helps teams identify whether their ERP is becoming a reporting bottleneck.

## What this checklist helps identify

- Spreadsheet dependency
- Slow access to operational answers
- Fragmented data across departments
- Weak project-margin visibility
- Lack of safe natural-language access

## Intended use

This checklist is educational. It is not a substitute for an ERP audit, security review, financial control review, or implementation plan.
